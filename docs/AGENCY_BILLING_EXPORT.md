# Agency formula billing export

This implementation supersedes the limited compatibility approach in BILLING_FORMULA_REVIEW.md. The user selected **Use agency formulas for billing**. The main billing worksheet uses the agency's formulas. Saved payroll and payslips remain authoritative employee payment records and appear separately in Payroll Reconciliation.

## Formula groups and mapping

All formula cells in ordinary employee row 6 are translated to each actual employee row using PhpSpreadsheet's reference translator. No sample employee identity is retained. Inputs come from the existing selected-run billing snapshot, which is built from actual deployment/site, verified DTR, payroll computation, approved overtime, holidays, approved Add Back, allowances and contribution tables. Finalized payroll snapshots are not modified or recalculated into employee pay during export.

- A: template sequence formula. B-D: selected employee name, designation and deployment area, written as literal text.
- E/F: selected snapshot daily rate and days worked. G: basic salary formula.
- H: number of unique Sunday duty dates from the production payroll result, a day count for AT/AU. It is no longer populated with a peso amount. A legacy snapshot without itemized Sunday dates contributes zero documented Sunday dates.
- I/K: agency regular OT rate and amount formulas. J: approved regular OT hours from the existing payroll result.
- L: production late/undertime minutes. M/N: template late deduction and adjusted salary formulas.
- O: regular allowance plus the existing Sunday allowance. P: template five-day incentive accrual. Q: approved Add Back.
- R/T/V/X: recorded holiday duty and approved holiday OT hours, split using the production holiday classifications. S/U/W/Y/Z: agency legal/special holiday and OT formulas.
- AA/AB/AD: agency additional earnings and gross formulas. AC: existing additional-late input, retained as an input; the source AD formula does not subtract it.
- AE: employee SSS. AF: employee Pag-IBIG plus the recorded additional Pag-IBIG deduction. AG: explicit additional PhilHealth input, if present. AH: employee PhilHealth. AI: employee Pag-IBIG plus explicitly recorded extra Pag-IBIG input. AJ: the source AG+AH+AI contribution summary. AK: recorded other deduction. AL/AM: agency total deduction and net formulas.
- AN: employer SSS regular contribution. AO: employer EC. AP: employer SSS MPF. AQ/AR: original formulas linked to employee contribution inputs. AS: original employer contribution total.
- AT/AU: original thirteenth-month and fixed 36.50-per-day overhead formulas. AV/AW: original employer additions and row client charges.
- Totals: actual source SUM columns preserved, including the source's intentionally blank AQ total.
- Billing summary: original basic, OT, allowance, Add Back, incentive, holiday, late, thirteenth-month, overhead, contribution, VAT, withholding, other-charge and grand-total formulas are moved by the template row-resize operation. The run's TotalInvoice is set from the resulting agency grand total, not from the saved payroll invoice estimate.
- Reconciliation: saved gross, net and original invoice amounts, agency gross/net/row charges, and formula differences for each signed employee. Saved amounts are snapshots; this sheet does not feed agency calculations. Row charges exclude the agency summary VAT/withholding; the headings state this distinction.

## Dynamic employees and special row

The source has ten ordinary employee rows (6-15) followed by an unnamed exception row 16. That row has distinct W and other expressions but no employee identity or daily rate. It remains unassigned and moves after the actual roster. It is never used as the eleventh employee. Source totals and summary references move with row insertion/removal. Zero employees retain one empty styled ordinary row and calculate zero, without sample amounts. Additional ordinary rows retain each column's own native formatting and normal row height.

## Export and import protection

All fresh Accounting exports continue through `Api/Accounting/export_client_billing.php`, using `client_billing_render_spreadsheet()` in `Api/Accounting/Lib/client_billing_spreadsheet.php`. The payroll table and modal download links already use this route. Historical downloads continue serving the saved historical artifact; they are not rewritten.

Authentication, active Accounting/Admin authorization, company/site roster isolation, mismatched-reference rejection, signed manifests, existing export transactions and the billing review/finalization flow remain in place. XLSX is written with cached calculated values and automatic/forced recalculation enabled. Downloading does not finalize payroll.

The signed snapshot records the agency billing basis and reconciliation cells. Import validates all reconciliation values/formulas and rejects added cells. Existing two-sheet historical exports remain supported; new exports have billing, reconciliation and the hidden manifest. Formula changes in uploaded workbooks are rejected, and uploaded formulas are not executed.

For agency exports, reviewed numeric edits are translated as deltas against saved payroll components, so a pre-existing agency-versus-payroll variance is never silently applied as a revision. AN/AO and AF map to the actual contribution components. H, AJ, AQ and AR are day-count/derived fields and require changes to their source inputs rather than ambiguous direct payroll revisions. Other edits still go through the existing preview and confirmation workflow.

## Discrepancies retained explicitly

The user authorized these agency report formulas, not changes to statutory payroll calculation:

- Ordinary W uses only the 30% special-holiday premium, while the unused exception W includes 130%.
- AL sums AE+AF, excluding PhilHealth, AJ and other deductions from its net calculation. AS excludes AQ employer PhilHealth. Both are preserved exactly and can differ from saved payroll.
- S/U and special OT use fixed agency rates; production can distinguish unworked holiday eligibility, rest days and double holidays. Unworked regular-holiday guarantees have zero worked hours and therefore are not reproduced by this agency duty-hours formula.
- P adds incentive accrual into agency gross; production may treat that accrual separately.
- AU uses 36.50 times F+H, replacing the former percentage billing estimate for the report. AT also uses F+H; F retains the production days-worked count and H the recorded Sunday-day count, so any overlap follows the agency formula.
- Agency row AW excludes summary VAT and withholding. Summary VAT is 12% of overhead and withholding is 2% of overhead.
- The special row's employee-category meaning and the agency's intentional contribution exclusions remain Accounting business-rule questions. No employee/category was invented for that row.
