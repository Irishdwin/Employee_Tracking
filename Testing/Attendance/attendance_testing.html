Attendance Testing Dashboard

Creates real records in the existing attendance / daily_time_records tables so you can test the Employee Dashboard, Smart Calendar, Team Lead review, DTR submission, Accounting verification, and Payroll — without touching phpMyAdmin. Admin login required.

1. Select who this record is for

Employee Payroll Rate — Actual Employee Test

Rates are read-only and re-resolved by the server. The selected worked scenario uses an anonymous disposable fixture with this employee's rates and schedule; no real holiday, attendance or salary is changed. Allowances and deductions are excluded from the compensation comparison.

Controlled Holiday Pay — Production End-to-End Test

Runs scenarios A–K and a preceding-holiday boundary check at ₱1,000/day (₱125/hour). Uses synthetic employees in a disposable database, then calls the current production Attendance, Team Lead DTR, Accounting verification and payroll code. No employee selection or real holiday this month is needed.

2. Payroll Test Scenario

Pick a scenario to pre-fill the fields below, or choose Custom and fill them in yourself.

The current holiday schema supports two distinct Regular Holidays on one date. Use the controlled suite above to verify this safely; manual presets do not create holidays in the official calendar.

3. Attendance Details

Sets Time In this many minutes after the scheduled start. The employee's grace period still applies. Leave blank to enter Time In manually.

Leave Time Out blank to simulate "still clocked in." To simulate an Absent day, don't add a record for that date (or delete an existing one below) — Absent is inferred automatically on past scheduled working days with no record. Non-working days for this employee's schedule are automatically Rest Days, regardless of what you enter here.

Set a Date above to look up holiday information.

5. Overtime Details

Creates a real, pre-approved row in the existing overtime table for this employee/date — the same table/ columns the Team Lead overtime tool uses, so it is picked up by the real payroll computation. Don't combine this with a Time Out on the attendance record above that already exceeds 8 working hours — production adds both sources together and will double-count the extra hours. This section is disabled when your Attendance Details above already imply more than 8 hours.
